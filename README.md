# VRC OSC Sender

A lightweight Windows app for sending VRChat avatar parameters over OSC (Open Sound Control)

---

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11
- [.NET 8 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/en-us/download/dotnet/8.0)

---

## Installation

1. Download `VrcOscSender.zip` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest)
2. Extract anywhere on your PC
3. Run `VrcOscSender.exe`

---

## Enabling OSC in VRChat

OSC must be enabled in VRChat before the app can communicate with it.

1. Open your **Action Menu** in VRChat
2. Go to **Options → OSC → Enabled**

---
